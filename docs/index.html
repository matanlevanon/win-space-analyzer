Why it works

Everything you need to free up space — fast

No digging through folders by hand. See the big picture, drill to the source, and clean up.

Seriously fast scanning

A parallel robocopy-based engine scans a full drive in ~30 seconds — about 3.5× faster than a regular scan.

Visual treemap

Every folder is a tile sized by weight — spot the big ones at a glance, click to dive in.

Biggest folders

A flat list of the worst space hogs across the whole drive, with one-click jump — no manual digging.

Compare with the previous scan

See exactly what grew, what freed up, and what’s new since last time — ongoing drive tracking.

Saved scans

Every drive remembers its last scan — jump back to the results instantly, no rescan needed.

Safe — deletes nothing

The app only finds and shows. Every item has an “Open in Explorer” button — deleting stays in your hands.

Three steps

From install to free space

1

Install

One irm … | iex command in PowerShell, or a regular Setup download. Installs like any app.

2

Scan a drive

Pick a drive (or “Scan all”) and wait ~30 seconds. The result is saved for next time.

3

Find & free up

Dive into the treemap, spot the heavy folder, and hit “Open in Explorer” to delete it yourself.
